- states anything the source doesn't contain;
- changes what the source says: upgrades, downgrades, narrows, contradicts, or uses an outdated figure;
- refuses or deflects when the source holds the answer; or
- breaks a tone rule the source sets.

**PASS** otherwise, including a refusal the source requires.
